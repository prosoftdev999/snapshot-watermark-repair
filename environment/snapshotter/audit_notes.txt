Incident: snap-2291

The consistency auditor rejected the reconstruction after comparing it with
replica readback. The failure is not isolated to one key: several node clock
boundaries, retried writes and watermark claims disagree with readback.

Operations also saved a sparse independent readback sample in
/app/data/readback_samples.jsonl. It is not a complete snapshot and it does not
say how reconstruction is supposed to work, but it is useful for falsifying a
candidate interpretation. /app/snapshotter/check_readback.py compares a current
/app/output.json against those samples and reports disagreements. That helper is
for investigation only; the grader does not use it and a clean sample check does
not prove the full answer is correct.

Operations asked for a clean reconstruction from the captured evidence before
re-cutting the snapshot. Treat reconstruct.py as the implementation that was
running during the incident, not as ground truth.

Replica comparison narrowed one discrepancy to retry collapsing when two accepted
copies share the same corrected delivery instant. The retry coordinator's rule is
deterministic: after timestamps are corrected to coordinator time, keep the earliest
delivered copy; if corrected delivery times tie, keep the lexicographically smaller
op_id. Source position is not a tie-break. The sparse readback remains useful as an
independent cross-check, but it is intentionally incomplete.
